About

I am a graduate researcher in psychology focused on social identity, self-categorization, uncertainty-identity theory, and leadership. My work asks how hidden psychological mechanisms shape public problems, from institutional reform to political persuasion.

I am interested in research and writing that connects rigorous social psychology with policy, governance, and public decision-making.
